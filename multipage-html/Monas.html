Description of Monas

Monument Nasional or usually better known as Monas is one of Indonesia's most iconic landmarks, it is a symbol of the struggle for independence. It sits at 132 meters tall, located in Merdeka Square the tower has a flame like sculpture plated in gold representing the will of freedom. The base houses a historical museum where visitors can enter and see the displays picturing Indonesia's fight for independence, inside there is an elevator where visitors can pay to ride to the observation deck, Surrounded by a large park that serves as a place where both tourist and locals gather.
